TinyBoard is a Bluetooth keyboard and mouse proxy that lets you control another device from your Mac. It uses a [Raytac MDBT50Q-RX](https://www.raytac.com/product/ins.php?index_id=89) along with some custom firmware to emulate a standard USB input device.

![Photo of an TinyBoard plugged into a MiSTer](/images/mister.jpg)

<p style="text-align: center">The TinyBoard is perfect for controlling devices like the MiSTer or Raspberry Pi on-the-go</p>
